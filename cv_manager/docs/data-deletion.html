How to request deletion

Fill in the form below, then use Open in email to create a pre-filled message to us. We verify ownership of the account (for example, by using the same email you use in the app) before deleting data, where reasonable and permitted by law. You can also write manually to the same address with subject: CV Manager — data deletion.

If your device does not open a mail app, copy the form contents into an email to jaeereyreedeso@gmail.com.

What we delete

When your request is confirmed, we delete or anonymize the personal data associated with your request, using our systems (Firebase Auth and Firestore) and any legal obligations.

In-app deletion

You can delete individual CVs in the app where that is offered. If full account removal is not available in your app version, use the email process above.
