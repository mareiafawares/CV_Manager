1. Overview

CV Manager (“we”, “us”) is a mobile application that lets you create and manage professional CVs. This policy describes how we handle information when you use the app, including services provided through Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and on Android, analytics).

2. Data we process

Depending on how you use the app, we may process: account and sign-in data (for example, email and authentication identifiers for email/password sign-in), CV and profile information you enter (such as name, contact details, work history, and skills), community and engagement data (for example, public CV settings, likes, and in-app notifications), and technical data needed to run the app (for example, app version, diagnostics, or security-related data processed by our providers).

3. Purposes

We use this data to provide, secure, and improve the service, including sign-in, storing and syncing your CVs, community features, and where applicable analytics to understand stability and usage.

4. Firebase and other providers

Authentication, the database (Cloud Firestore), and on Android, analytics, use Google Firebase. The app may load fonts from Google (e.g. via the google_fonts package) for display. Processing of information by these services is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and related terms, in addition to this policy.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service, unless a longer period is required by law. You may request deletion of your account or data by following the process on our Data Deletion page, or you may use in-app features to remove specific content (such as individual CVs) where available.

6. Your rights

Where applicable, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us at the email address above.
